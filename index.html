About Dan

A Tutor Who Understands the Pressure

I'm Dan, a maths tutor based in Milton Keynes with an MBA and a background in technology and AI.

I came to tutoring through a personal experience that changed how I think about learning. When my son was preparing for his 11+ exam, I spent months working with him, finding ways to explain maths that made genuine sense to him. Building real understanding with real-life examples. He went on to score 132 in the 11+.

That experience taught me something: the right approach, the right pace, and the right relationship between a student and their tutor makes an enormous difference — not just to results, but to how a young person feels about themselves.

I'm patient by nature, structured by training, and Enhanced DBS checked. Every student is capable of improving. They often just need someone who explains things differently, and who treats every small win as worth celebrating.

I want to help as many children to understand maths, which I believe to be a true life skill. My current student cohort is a brilliant example of young minds exploring maths.

If your child is struggling, anxious about maths, or simply not reaching their potential, I'd love to have a chat.
